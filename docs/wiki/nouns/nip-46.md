# NIP-46

A protocol bifurcated into (1) pure crypto/message format for NIP-44 encryption and JSON-RPC envelopes, and (2) a relay-connected RPC loop mechanism for communicating with remote signers; the transport/execution layer (threads, sockets, reconnect) is platform-owned, not protocol-owned
