# Ingest Chokepoint Unification

## Architectural Bug

Issue #1440 (ghost-post / missing local echo for non-replaceable events) and #1442 (persistence entangled with relevance, causing the authoritative EventStore to have relevance-shaped holes where kind:1/6 are the only persistence-gated kinds while all others persist unconditionally via `verify_and_persist`) share one root cause: persistence, admission/relevance filtering, and projection/cache mutation are fused in the same functions and duplicated across two hand-maintained per-kind dispatch ladders (`handle_event` for relay events, `record_local_publish_intent` for local events) that drift when synced by hand. The local-publish ladder (`record_local_publish_intent`) has no arms for non-replaceable kinds (kind:1, kind:6, kind:7, etc.), causing locally-published notes, replies, reposts, and reactions to be invisible until a relay echoes them back. The fix must separate persistence, admission/relevance, and projection-mutation into three distinct layers that never bleed into each other; the product must not branch on NIP kind numbers in the ingest path, because per-kind dispatch arms indicate a mixing of concerns or abstraction layers. A locally-published event and its relay-echoed event are the same event and must converge on one kind-agnostic ingest chokepoint rather than two parallel per-kind dispatch ladders that drift. The relay ingest pipeline currently has two distinct arms for non-replaceables: timeline kinds (kind:1, kind:6) go through `ingest_timeline_event` (which self-containedly does store.insert, dispatcher fan-out, timeline read-cache append, and notify_event_observers), while other non-replaceables (kind:7, etc.) go through `verify_and_persist` plus a separate `notify_event_observers` call. Kind:1 and kind:6 events must be routed through `verify_and_persist` (eliminating their duplicate sig-verify and store.insert inside ingest_timeline_event) so that the chokepoint is genuinely uniform for all kinds. The #1440 suggested fix has two technical flaws: calling both `verify_and_persist` and `ingest_timeline_event` causes a double store-insert and double dispatcher fan-out, and calling `notify_event_observers` after `ingest_timeline_event` (which already fires observers) causes a double observer fire violating the D4 single-fire invariant. Two independent analyses (Opus agent and codex exec) converged on the same architectural verdict: persistence/admission/projection conflation is the root cause, and the fix is a unified kind-agnostic chokepoint with caches demoted to observers. The bug consists of three currently-conflated concerns that must be separated: (a) the ingest chokepoint (verify sig, persist for non-ephemeral, dispatch to EventIngestDispatcher, fire notify_event_observers — kind-agnostic, gated on Inserted|Replaced|Ephemeral; all valid events including ephemeral must reach NIP parsers and app-facing observers, with only the persist step excluding ephemeral kinds 20000–29999; persistence is gated by the store outcome: only Inserted|Replaced outcomes are persisted (non-ephemeral canonical writes); Duplicate|Superseded|Tombstoned|Rejected do not persist or deliver; Duplicate echoes are projection-silent but must still bump relay_count for diagnostics), (b) admission (valid signature and non-ephemeral kind — acquisition-match is deleted, not generalized; for any event from a connected relay, admission requires only a valid signature and non-ephemeral kind; local publishes are unconditionally admitted as Provenance::Local; 'non-ephemeral' qualifies only the persist step, not delivery — admission answers 'did I ask for this or did I make it?' — a trust boundary on untrusted relay input, not 'do I want to display this' (relevance) or 'is this worth keeping' (persistence/GC)), and (c) relevance/projections as observers reacting to (a). Today these are conflated inside per-kind ingest arms and duplicated across the two ladders. Persistence must not bake a social-app assumption (timeline_authors = follow set) into the storage layer — a third-party NMP app declaring a non-social interest must get its events stored like any other. The timeline read-cache (`self.events`/`self.timeline`) is a second, in-memory, lossy store separate from the authoritative LMDB EventStore and hard-wired into the ingest function instead of being an observer — this is the abstraction-layer mixing that the fix must resolve. The authoritative EventStore is supposed to be a complete, GC-bounded log of every validly-signed non-ephemeral event the kernel ingests; a throwaway cache's policy (the follow-set-driven timeline read-cache) must not punch holes in the source of truth. ADR-0057 is the durable, self-contained authority for the unified ingest chokepoint decision; it does not depend on temporal plan files (docs/plans/arch-fixes.md) as authorities — those are WIP planning artifacts marked for deletion on merge. ADR-0042's framing of `should_store_event` as store admission is corrected in place (wrong content removed, not annotated as superseded) with an Amendment block explaining the demotion to a read-time projection predicate. GitHub issue #1442 tracks the architectural root cause (persistence entangled with relevance — authoritative EventStore has relevance-shaped holes), cross-referencing #1440 as the local-publish symptom; #1440's 'add another arm' framing is architecturally wrong and superseded by the unified ingest fix. Issue #1443 tracks research into unbounded local event cache (never lose fetched events; is LMDB RAM-bound or disk-bound?) as a follow-up. The three durable docs (subsystems.md, 08-eventstore.md, 12-publish-and-ledger.md) were verified as already consistent with the new model; no should_store_event-as-admission framing existed in them, so no edits were needed. The EventIngestDispatcher (per-NIP parser registry) is the D0 substrate-honest mechanism: NIP crates register parsers for their kinds at composition time, parsers write only to their own NIP crate's caches, and the kernel reads back through narrow capability traits without ever naming a NIP kind literal directly; kind knowledge lives in the NIP crates that register parsers. NIP crates (e.g. nmp-nip65 Kind10002Parser, nmp-nip17 Kind10050Parser) follow a CQRS/read-model pattern: parsers distill each event into a compact typed cache the NIP crate owns, and the kernel reads back through narrow capability traits without ever knowing what kind produced the data. NIP crate caches (DmRelayCache, MailboxCache, etc.) are derived projections rebuilt from the EventStore on restart; if an event was dropped at ingest due to the relevance gate, its derived cache cannot be rebuilt after restart, causing silent failures like missing DMs — so a relevance-shaped hole in the store becomes a permanent hole in every derived projection that replays from it. This is why persist-everything is non-negotiable for projection correctness. Full ingest-path D0 (zero kind literals) lands only after PR 3; PR 1 only removes the relevance persistence gate and achieves partial D0 (persistence stops assuming 'social'). The `handle_event` `match event.Kind { 0|3|1|6 => }` arms are a latent D0 smell that the unification removes. Workstreams D (signer/capability authority) and E (action/projection lifecycle) were carved into a separate sibling plan (arch-authority-lifecycle.md) because they are orthogonal to the event-flow trinity (ingest/acquire/publish).

## Admission/Relevance Gate Misplacement

should_store_event gates persistence inconsistently: it applies only to kind:1 notes and kind:6 reposts (routed through ingest_timeline_event), making kind:1 notes the only kind that must prove relevance before being persisted, while every other kind goes through verify_and_persist which has no relevance gate and stores any validly-signed event unconditionally — the gate is kind-asymmetric and incoherent. The `should_store_event` clause `timeline_authors.contains(&event.pubkey)` smuggles follow-set relevance into persistence admission and is the specific clause that blocks self-authored notes; it must be demoted to a pure read-time timeline-VIEW predicate consulted only by the timeline-cache observer to decide what enters the timeline view — it must never gate store.insert or notify_event_observers. A false result from should_store_event only skips timeline projection, not store.insert. A user is normally not in their own follow set, so self-authored notes fail the should_store_event admission gate and are dropped or parked in pre_kind3_buffer; a naive reuse of `ingest_timeline_event` silently fails for fresh root notes. The user confirmed that should_store_event gating storage by follow-set membership makes no sense, prompting the deeper architectural investigation. The authoritative EventStore currently has relevance-shaped holes because should_store_event gates persistence for kind:1/6, while kind:0, kind:3, kind:7, and all other kinds go through verify_and_persist which stores unconditionally — this falsifies cache-serve/offline soundness, projection rebuildability, cross-session dedup, and makes storage bounding double-policied and incoherent. Every non-ephemeral event the kernel ingests is cached (persisted) regardless of kind or relevance, with the sole exception of ephemeral kinds (20000–29999) which are excluded at the store layer per NIP-16; that exclusion belongs at the store layer, not at admission. Persistence is gated by the store outcome: only Inserted|Replaced outcomes are persisted (non-ephemeral canonical writes); Duplicate|Superseded|Tombstoned|Rejected do not persist or deliver. Durable event storage must be unconditional for valid non-ephemeral events; relevance (follow lists, timeline authors, open interests) must gate only timeline/feed/read projections, not persistence. Every validly-signed non-ephemeral event (canonical outcomes `Inserted | Replaced`) the kernel ingests is persisted unconditionally regardless of the author's relevance; the single storage bound is pin-aware LRU GC, not a relevance gate. The authoritative EventStore must be a complete, GC-bounded log of every validly-signed non-ephemeral event the kernel ingests, with relevance/filtering applied only at read/projection time, not at write/persistence time; storage is bounded by GC/watermark/pin-aware LRU alone. Admission to the ingest chokepoint requires only a valid signature and non-ephemeral kind — acquisition-match is deleted, not generalized. The admission question is purely 'did I ask for this, or did I make it?' — a trust boundary on untrusted relay input, not 'do I want to display this' (relevance) or 'is this worth keeping' (persistence/GC) — and has nothing to do with kind or follow set. Admission requires a valid signature and non-ephemeral kind; 'non-ephemeral' qualifies only the persist step, not delivery. A relay sending events that were not explicitly requested is not a problem — signatures prevent forgery, unsolicited events are hidden by read-time projections, and DoS from bulk unsolicited events is a transport-layer quota concern, not an ingest admission gate. Relay admission means the event came with a valid signature; it must not inspect follow lists, timeline authors, app view state, or projection relevance. The ADR (PR 0) no longer needs to resolve a loose-vs-strict admission-semantics decision because admission = valid sig + non-ephemeral kind (all valid events including ephemeral reach parsers/observers; non-ephemeral qualifies only persistence) is the complete gate; dropping acquisition-match dissolves the ambiguity. should_store_event genuinely belongs to the read-cache projection ('should this show up in my timeline view?'), but because persistence and projection live in one function (ingest_timeline_event), the gate ends up blocking the authoritative store insert too; admission/relevance got entangled with persistence only for the kind that flows through the timeline function. The subscription's REQ filter is already the relevance contract (you asked the relay for these events); re-filtering them at write time is redundant, kind-asymmetric, and is exactly why a self-authored published note (no subscription, author not in their own follow set) falls through the floor. The authoritative EventStore is a bounded local cache (~10k hot-event ceiling, pin-aware LRU eviction), not an infinite log; 'persist everything' means admission is not relevance-gated and eviction is the single principled bound, not that events are kept forever. GC (pin-aware LRU eviction to HOT_EVENT_CEILING=10000) is the single principled storage bound replacing the relevance gate; newly-stored non-followed events are unpinned and evicted first, so removing the relevance gate does not cause unbounded growth. Locally-published events are pinned (via a publish-in-flight pin source in derive_store_pin_set) while in the publish queue until first relay confirmation or terminal settlement, preventing LRU eviction before the relay echo arrives and deduplicates. GC is wired and structurally adequate: run_gc_step fires every 60s on the actor idle tick with a 2k events/50ms budget, HOT_EVENT_CEILING=10,000 and unpinned-first LRU eviction; the prior 'gc_step never called in production' finding is stale/fixed by #1069. The safety property is pin-set correctness ensuring live events are never evicted, and eviction is the single principled storage bound. NIP crate caches (e.g., DmRelayCache, MailboxCache) are derived projections rebuilt from the EventStore on restart; if a kind:10050 event was dropped at ingest (never persisted), the DmRelayCache can't be rebuilt, read_relays() returns None, and DM publish fails closed — this is why the authoritative store must be a complete log. Storage bounding must be governed exclusively by GC and watermarks alone (the one principled mechanism), not by any ad-hoc relevance gate that pre-empts GC; the current double-policy of both relevance-gating and GC is incoherent and must be eliminated. pre_kind3_buffer is deleted in the fix; once everything is persisted, a late kind:3 just makes the timeline observer re-scan the store and nothing was ever lost. The clean model for ingest is: persist anything you subscribed to or authored, unconditionally and kind-agnostically; decide 'is this in my timeline view' at the projection layer; this dissolves both #1440 and the kind-asymmetric gating at once. Issue #1442 was filed as the architectural bug report: 'Persistence is entangled with relevance — authoritative EventStore has relevance-shaped holes (architectural),' labeled bug/category:violation/priority:p1, cross-referencing #1440 as the local-publish half of the same root cause. Issue #1442 must document six blast-radius consequences: cache-serve/offline is structurally broken, projections are not rebuildable, no cross-session coverage floor causes re-REQ amplification, storage bounding is double-policied and incoherent, determinism/replay hostility (D9), and the kernel hard-codes a social-app assumption (D0 violation). Issue #1443 was filed: 'Research: unbounded local event cache' as a follow-up to investigate making the durable store unbounded (LMDB is memory-mapped/disk-bound) while bounding only the RAM working set. The truncation→usize::MAX (LRU-skip) fallback path in derive_store_gc_inputs is the one place where unbounded persistence could outrun a disabled LRU and must be verified in the PR's test plan.

## Current State of the Chokepoint

All event sources (relay, local publish, cache replay) route through a single kind-agnostic ingest chokepoint (`ingest_accepted_event`, built on `verify_and_persist`) that performs verify, persist, dispatch, and notify, replacing the two hand-maintained per-kind, per-source ingest ladders (`handle_event` relay arms and `record_local_publish_intent` local arms). verify_and_persist is the near-chokepoint candidate; it already does sig-verify, store.insert with provenance+dedup, raw-tap fan-out, EventIngestDispatcher fan-out, and replaceable TTL stamping kind-agnostically, but does not fire notify_event_observers (callers do per-arm). The unified chokepoint verifies the signature, persists via store.insert (non-ephemeral only, Inserted|Replaced), dispatches to the EventIngestDispatcher, and fires notify_event_observers, all gated on Inserted|Replaced|Ephemeral — if verify_and_persist fires notify_event_observers itself and the caches (self.profiles, self.seed_contacts, timeline read-cache) are registered observers fed by the single chokepoint, it becomes genuinely kind-agnostic. Ephemeral events (20000–29999) are excluded from persistence at the store layer only but must be dispatched to NIP parsers and notified to app observers — they reach the app, they just aren't stored. The prior code had verify_and_persist almost as the chokepoint but it did not fire notify_event_observers itself (callers did per-arm) and was bypassed by kind:1/6 which used a parallel self-contained function (`ingest_timeline_event`) with its own duplicate `store.insert` and sig-verify; the unification routes kind:1/6 through the same `verify_and_persist` and demotes the timeline-cache append to an observer. The wildcard arm's notify_event_observers previously fired only on Inserted|Replaced, excluding Ephemeral — a latent bug where ephemeral events never reached app-facing KernelEventObservers even though they reached NIP parsers; the unified chokepoint fixes this by moving notify_event_observers inside verify_and_persist and gating on Inserted|Replaced|Ephemeral. Persistence writes only the non-ephemeral subset (Inserted|Replaced) to the authoritative EventStore; Ephemeral returns InsertOutcome::Ephemeral and is not written. Persistence is gated by the store outcome: only Inserted|Replaced outcomes are persisted (non-ephemeral canonical writes); Duplicate|Superseded|Tombstoned|Rejected do not persist or deliver. Delivery (dispatch to NIP parsers + notify to app observers) fires exactly once on Inserted|Replaced|Ephemeral; Duplicate|Superseded|Tombstoned|Rejected are projection-silent to preserve D4 single-fire, but kind:1/6 must still bump the cached `relay_count` on `Duplicate` for diagnostics. The unified post-store fan-out helper `project_accepted_event` is shared by both the live chokepoint (`ingest_accepted_event`) and the cache-serve replay path (`feed_served_event`), ensuring ADR-0045 compliance where cache-serve never calls `store.insert` but runs the same projection/notify seam; NIP-parser dispatch, transition sweeps, D9 clamping, and observer notify all execute identically regardless of source. Relay events enter after relay-frame bookkeeping at `ingest/mod.rs:281→282` (the clean split point — lines 252–281 are relay-only bookkeeping: JSON parse, relay counters, transport provenance, wire-sub diagnostics), local publishes enter with `local://publish` provenance, and cache replay feeds `feed_served_event` per ADR-0045. The unified ingest pipeline has three layers: Layer 1 Admission per-source (relay admitted on valid signature and non-ephemeral kind, local admitted unconditionally, cache replay trivially admitted), Layer 2 The chokepoint (one kind-agnostic function: verify sig, store.insert, dispatch to parser registry, notify observers), Layer 3 Projections as observers (where kind and relevance finally live). Admission to the chokepoint requires a valid signature and non-ephemeral kind — acquisition-match is deleted, not generalized. The single kind-agnostic ingest chokepoint sits below handle_event's relay-only bookkeeping (lines 252–280 of ingest/mod.rs: JSON parse, relay counters, transport provenance, wire-sub diagnostics, claim_expansion_match_author); local publish and cache replay call it directly with provenance and claim_match_author=None, not through handle_event. handle_event must not receive local publishes directly because it performs relay-frame bookkeeping (relay counters, transport provenance, wire-sub diagnostics) that a local publish has no relay frame for; the shared seam sits below that bookkeeping. The chokepoint performs steps kind-agnostically in order: verify signature, persist (store.insert, non-ephemeral only), dispatch to per-NIP parser registry, notify observers — all valid events including ephemeral reach parsers and observers, with only the persist step excluding ephemerals. Observer fan-out (notify_event_observers) lives inside the canonical persistence path (behind the insert result for non-ephemeral, unconditionally for ephemeral via an `Inserted|Replaced|Ephemeral` gate), not manually wired from relay and local-publish callers. The EventIngestDispatcher dispatches to per-NIP IngestParsers as the D0-honest mechanism: parsers are registered per kind at composition time by NIP crates, parsers write only to their own NIP crate's caches, and the kernel dispatches accepted events and reads back through narrow capability traits without naming NIP kinds directly; kind knowledge lives in the NIP crates that register parsers. ## Unification Fix

The architecturally correct fix is to collapse to one kind-agnostic ingest chokepoint and demote the caches to observers: a single kind-agnostic ingest chokepoint (`ingest_accepted_event`) replaces the two hand-maintained per-kind, per-source ingest ladders (`handle_event` relay arms and `record_local_publish_intent` local arms). `verify_and_persist` is the single kind-agnostic ingest chokepoint (`ingest_accepted_event`), replacing the two hand-maintained per-kind, per-source ingest ladders. notify_event_observers is moved inside verify_and_persist (gated on Inserted|Replaced|Ephemeral for non-ephemeral and ephemeral events; all valid events including ephemeral must reach parsers and observers; on Duplicate outcome, delivery is projection-silent preserving D4 single-fire for relay echo of local publish, but must still bump the cached `relay_count` for diagnostics) so that observers fire exactly once per accepted event from one code location, and a relay echo of a locally-published event dedups to Duplicate and does not double-fire observers (D4 single-writer invariant), regardless of how many sibling relays echo it; demote self.profiles/self.seed_contacts/timeline read-cache/mailbox-DM caches to observers; decouple should_store_event from persistence, making it a pure read-time predicate consulted only by the timeline-cache observer to decide what enters the timeline view — a false result only skips timeline projection, not store.insert; admission to the chokepoint requires only a valid signature and non-ephemeral kind — acquisition-match is deleted, not generalized; tag local publishes as Provenance::Local (unconditionally admitted, treated as unconditionally relevant by the timeline-cache observer, giving read-your-writes for all kinds with no per-kind arm); and delete local_publish_intent.rs so local publishes route through the shared chokepoint with local provenance instead of a separate per-kind ladder. The chokepoint must not name NIP kind literals directly in ingest dispatch; it must gate by behavioral predicates only (is_replaceable, is_parameterized_replaceable, follow_feed_kinds.contains, registered-parser is_interested) per D0 substrate-honesty, and kind:1059 gift-wraps must stay excluded from local echo via the parser registry, not a kind literal. Publish behavior is classified by a single typed policy table (classify_publish_behavior) that is the only function permitted to compare a publish kind to a named KIND_* constant; raw kind-literal guards in publish/action.rs are replaced. PrivateFailClosed (kind:1059 gift-wrap, kind:14 sealed chat) is enforced structurally at the publish-engine chokepoint — every signed publish funnels through validate_publish_routing, and Auto/empty-Explicit for private kinds is rejected before any outbound frame/queue entry, with a D10 toast and action failure recorded. At the dispatch-emit site (dispatch_due), private-fail-closed kinds may only emit to a relay whose relay_reasons includes Explicit; all other relay selections are refused, the row is terminally finalized, and no frame is emitted. A doctrine-lint gate bans raw kind literals (==, match arms, matches!, contains) in publish routing outside policy.rs; non-vacuity is proven by injecting known violation shapes and asserting the detector fires. The diag-firehose sub_id escape hatch is preserved in the timeline projection gate (as a sub-scheme recognition the kernel already does, not a NIP literal) because the firehose is a kernel content-stress sub carrying kind:1 that tests and GC/pin stress paths rely on.
