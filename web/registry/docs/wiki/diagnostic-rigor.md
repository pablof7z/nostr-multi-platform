# Diagnostic Rigor

## Diagnostic Rigor

When a live observation contradicts a past success, it must be investigated as a potential regression, not dismissed as flakiness. Fabricated root-cause documents (citing nonexistent files or unobserved wire messages) must be retracted; diagnosis must be instrument-first with real captured output, not theorized. [^6a951-12]
